Start Your Astrophotography Journey

Astrophotography combines astronomy, photography, and technology to capture objects in the night sky. From photographing the Moon with a simple camera to capturing distant nebulae and galaxies through a telescope, there are many ways to explore this hobby. This website is designed to make the process easier to understand for beginners.
