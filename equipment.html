Astrophotography Equipment

Astrophotography equipment can range from a basic camera and tripod to telescopes and tracking mounts. Beginners do not need to purchase everything at once. The best equipment depends on what you want to photograph.

Camera

A DSLR or mirrorless camera with manual settings is a useful starting point for astrophotography. Being able to control exposure time, ISO, and focus gives you more control when photographing the night sky.

Tripod

A sturdy tripod keeps the camera stable during longer exposures. This is one of the simplest and most useful accessories for someone beginning astrophotography.

Tracking Mount

A tracking mount follows the apparent movement of the night sky as Earth rotates. This can allow photographers to use longer exposures while reducing star trails.

Telescope

Telescopes can be used to photograph smaller or more distant objects such as planets, nebulae, galaxies, and star clusters. A telescope is not required when first learning astrophotography.
